Engineered strength, designed for everyday use

Decora Industries manufactures and supplies steel furniture, storage systems and fencing solutions for businesses, institutions and homes.

We build practical, dependable steel products — from a single household almirah to complete warehouse racking and perimeter fencing — and we make them to your requirement.

Our approach

Manufacturing you can plan around

Every product moves through a consistent, controlled process — material selection, cutting and forming, welding, surface preparation, powder coating, inspection and dispatch. That repeatability is what lets us deliver quality across large and repeat orders.

We work with commercial procurement teams, dealers and distributors, and individual household customers alike — matching the right specification to each application.
